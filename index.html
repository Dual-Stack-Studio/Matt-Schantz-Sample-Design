I follow the light
and wait for it.

Hey there, my name is Matt. This is a home for some of the pictures and videos I have taken and worked on over the past five or so years. Most of it happens outside: long drives, early alarms, cold hands and a camera that gets heavier by the hour. The mission is simple: bring back the place the way it felt.
