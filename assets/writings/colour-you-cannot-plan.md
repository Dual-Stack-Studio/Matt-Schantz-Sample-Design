Hot springs paint themselves in rust, teal and amber, and they change with the temperature of the morning. A colour that was vivid at nine can be gone by noon.

Planning helps with where to stand and when to arrive. It does very little for the colour itself. That part is a conversation with the weather, and the weather does most of the talking.

![Yellowstone National Park, 10/26/24](matt/yellowstone-national-park-102624/09.jpg)

> Take the picture you are given, then look again.

Steam softens everything. It blurs the edges of a scene and lets one bright patch stand for the whole place. Photographs made in it tend to feel quieter than the moment felt, which is not a bad trade.
