The drive between two places is easy to treat as dead time. A number on a screen, a coffee, a playlist. It is often where the trip actually happens.

Slow roads give the day its shape. They pass fences, small towns, a lake that is not on any list, and they teach you to stop when something catches your eye instead of promising to come back.

## Stopping early

Every pull-off is a small decision. Most of the time nothing is there. Occasionally the whole valley opens up and the car is parked at exactly the right angle.

> The best frame of the day is rarely the one you drove toward.

Arriving late is a fair price. A day with room in it makes better pictures, and better stories, than a day that kept to the schedule.
