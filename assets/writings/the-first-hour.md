Most of a good photograph is decided before the sun is up. It is decided by the alarm, by the thermos, by the choice to walk a little further down the road than feels reasonable.

The first hour of light is a strange negotiation. The sky changes every few minutes, the colours arrive in the wrong order, and whatever was planned in the car turns out to be a suggestion. The only reliable strategy is to stay put and keep looking.

> Patience is not waiting. It is paying attention while nothing happens.

There is a moment, usually shorter than expected, when the light is soft enough to hold the whole scene. After that the contrast returns and the picture is just a picture. The goal is to be ready for the moment and relaxed about missing it.
